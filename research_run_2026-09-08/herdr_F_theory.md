## 1. Sanctioning under biased, unreliable blame

**Residual gap.** Failure attribution and LLM reputation systems now both exist, but no found work drives sanctions from a measured culprit-attribution channel and derives when the resulting institution is worse than no sanctioning. The residual novelty is not “reputation under noise”; classical and 2026 theory already occupies that. It is **role-correlated blame error over collaborative traces, coupled to future team selection**.

**Precise formulation.** Let a population \(N\) contain persistent agents with latent competence \(\theta_i\in[0,1]\). At round \(t\), a team \(K_t\) of size \(k\) is sampled according to selection probabilities \(p_t(r)\), where \(r_t\in\mathbb R^N\) is reputation. A team trajectory is generated by a cooperative POSG and ends in \(Y_t\in\{0,1\}\). Conditional on failure, a latent decisive culprit \(C_t\in K_t\) is drawn from a structural failure model \(P(C_t\mid K_t,\theta,x_t)\). The attributor emits \(B_t\) through an error kernel

\[
E_{bj}(x_t)=P(B_t=b\mid C_t=j,x_t),
\]

where \(x_t\) contains role, verbosity, turn position, and topology. Reputation updates as \(r_{i,t+1}=\phi(r_{i,t},\mathbf 1\{B_t=i\},Y_t)\); sanctions act through \(p_{t+1}\), exclusion, or payoff loss. Welfare is long-run task value minus exclusion and attribution costs:

\[
W_s(E,\phi)=\liminf_{T\to\infty}\frac1T\mathbb E\sum_{t=1}^T
[Y_t-c_A-c_S].
\]

Define \(a^*=\inf\{a:W_s(E(a),\phi)\ge W_0\}\), but do not collapse \(E\) to scalar accuracy in the main theorem: two kernels with equal \(a\) can have opposite welfare effects.

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** Prove for a two-type population that (i) a unique welfare threshold exists under uniform off-diagonal error; (ii) \(a^*\) rises with sanction severity and team size under regularity conditions; and (iii) a sufficiently role-biased kernel makes expected reputation negatively correlated with competence, so blame-sanctioning is worse than matched-rate random exclusion. For conformal sets \(S_t\), derive a sufficient safety condition in terms of coverage, expected set size, and sanction allocation. Coverage alone cannot guarantee welfare: this is an important impossibility statement.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** At the measured Who&When operating point (53.5% agent-level accuracy), role/verbosity-biased blame produces lower steady-state success than matched-rate random exclusion; conformal abstention restores nonnegative welfare at a nontrivial sanction rate. Refute if the empirical error kernel is approximately exchangeable or sanctioning dominates random exclusion across the measured region.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Classical noisy/unfair-reputation phase-transition models already derive critical noise or liar fractions, and 2026 indirect-reciprocity work explicitly studies perception noise [S18, S19]. RepuNet already couples reputation to network rewiring [S15]. Novelty dies if one of these models already permits trace-derived, role-dependent culprit kernels and proves the same competence-signal inversion. The live check did not find that coupling. Primary anchors: [S15–S19].

## 2. Endogenous adaptive verification as a delayed control loop

**Residual gap.** Costly verification behavior has been measured, delayed verification has a stability theory, and dynamic trust graphs exist. The open coupling is to identify an LLM trust-to-audit law from behavior, put it into a delayed closed loop, predict held-out transitions, and constrain it without spending more audits than a fixed policy.

**Precise formulation.** Use a mean-field POSG/control model with actionable-error prevalence \(q_t\), behavioral trust \(\tau_t\), audit action \(a_t\in[0,1]\), audit delay \(d_v\), evidence delay \(d_o\), and selective observation \(M_t\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(a_t)\):

\[
q_{t+1}=(1-a_{t-d_v})F_\lambda(q_t)+a_{t-d_v}G_\rho(q_t)+\xi_t,
\quad
\tau_{t+1}=H(\tau_t,M_{t-d_o}Y_{t-d_o},M_{t-d_o}),
\]

\[
a_t=g_\beta(\tau_t,c_v,c_e).
\]

Missing audits are missing labels, never successes. Fit \(H,g_\beta\) under randomized audits, then freeze them before the stability sweep. Linearization around a nonzero operating point yields a delayed matrix polynomial; STAV chooses the nearest raw action satisfying a robust spectral-radius margin over an uncertainty set.

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** (1) Exhibit a nonempty parameter region in which a cost-matched fixed \(a\equiv\bar a\) is Schur-stable but endogenous \(g_\beta\circ H\) is unstable. (2) Give a robust invariant-set or spectral-radius certificate and a bound on STAV’s extra audit cost relative to raw adaptation. (3) Prove **selective-label non-identifiability**: without exogenous/randomized audits, two error processes can induce the same observed audit history but opposite stability classifications. This makes experimental randomization a theorem requirement, not a methodological preference.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Independently fitted behavioral parameters predict stable/oscillatory/cascade cells held out by delay and topology better than a flexible descriptive baseline, and STAV dominates raw adaptation at exactly matched realized audit cost. Refute if randomization removes the history effect, plausible fitted parameters never enter the unstable region, or a rate limiter matches STAV.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Delayed Verification already proves delay thresholds and corrector-placement results [S11]; Trust Between AI Agents already measures costly verification dynamics [S10]; DySCo and DynaTrust occupy dynamic trust topology [S12, S14]. Novelty disappears if their full models already endogenize trust-dependent audit intensity and identify it from behavior. Current primary pages do not claim that. Primary anchors: [S10–S14].

## 3. Strategic shared memory as a polluted public good

**Residual gap.** Shared-memory systems govern access, provenance, staleness, and deletion, but contributors are benevolent. Sequential public-goods games model LLM effort, but not a persistent reusable artifact whose **quality** and **volume** can be chosen separately.

**Precise formulation.** In a dynamic Bayesian public-goods game, agent \(i\) has private curation cost \(c_i\), proprietary value \(v_i\), and task type \(z_{it}\). It chooses disclosure \(d_{it}\in\{0,1\}\), effort \(e_{it}\ge0\), and a memory payload \(m_{it}\). Latent quality \(q_{it}\sim P(q\mid e_{it},z_{it})\) affects future users through retrieval probabilities \(R_j(m_{it}\mid M_t)\). The pool transition is \(M_{t+1}=U(M_t,m_{it})\). Utility includes task reward, relative standing, read access, effort, and delayed externalities:

\[
u_i=\sum_t\delta^t\big[r_i(M_t)-c_i(e_{it})-v_i d_{it}+T_i(h_t)\big].
\]

A mechanism chooses transfers/access \(T_i\) using only retrievals and downstream outcomes, which are noisy and selectively observed.

**Theorem / impossibility / objective opportunity.** Characterize the neutral-pool Markov-perfect equilibrium and show under-provision of high-quality memories; with cheap low-effort deposits, the equilibrium may be pollution rather than silence. Prove that flat per-write payment increases volume but can reduce average quality. Under an identifiable causal contribution signal, derive an approximately incentive-compatible usage payment; without ground truth or conditional-independence assumptions, show that truthful quality revelation and budget balance cannot both be guaranteed. Optimize discounted social welfare subject to budget, participation, and read-capacity constraints.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Relative-performance incentives reduce the marginal value of deposited memories compared with a cooperative control; flat payments raise count but not downstream marginal value; outcome-conditioned access credits improve pool welfare and behavior changes with payoffs rather than honesty wording. Refute if instructions alone match the mechanism or strategic agents deposit memories of equal measured value.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** MAC-SPGG already makes contribution a unique SPNE but lacks persistence and separable quality [S23]. CARP is mechanism design under noisy unverifiable outcomes and behaviorally tests LLM incentives [S25]. GateMem and governed-memory work already formalize multi-principal governance [S20–S22]. Novelty dies if CARP’s complaint mechanism maps directly to persistent memory quality with downstream retrieval externalities, or if GateMem contains strategic contributor utilities; neither primary abstract claims this. Primary anchors: [S20–S25].

## 4. Decentralized-optimum regret decomposition for LLM teams

**Residual gap.** Optimum-grounded LLM collaboration exists only against a full-information/centralized solver. The defensible gap is evaluation against the exact **decentralized** optimum and separation of environment information loss from team implementation loss.

**Precise formulation.** For finite-horizon Dec-POMDP

\[
\mathcal M=\langle I,S,b_0,\{A_i,O_i\}_{i\in I},T,Z,R,H\rangle,
\]

let \(\Pi_{\mathrm{dec}}\) contain joint policies whose \(i\)-th action depends only on local action-observation history \(h_i^t\), and \(\Pi_{\mathrm{cent}}\) contain policies over the joint history. A rendering/channel \(L\) and protocol \(P\) induce an LLM policy \(\hat\pi_{L,P}\). Define

\[
D(\mathcal M)=V^*_{\mathrm{cent}}-V^*_{\mathrm{dec}},\qquad
E(\mathcal M,L,P)=V^*_{\mathrm{dec}}-V(\hat\pi_{L,P}).
\]

Normalize \(E\) against a declared random-policy value, not against \(V^*_{\mathrm{cent}}\).

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** The algebraic decomposition is trivial; the worthwhile theorem is **central-only non-identifiability**. Construct two task families with identical centralized value and identical observed LLM return but different \(V^*_{\mathrm{dec}}\), proving that any score using only a centralized oracle cannot distinguish intrinsic information loss from avoidable team loss. Add finite-sample confidence bounds for online LLM policies and exact values for emitted controllers. A second result can relate \(D(\mathcal M)\) to Blackwell ordering of joint observation structures; information refinement cannot increase decentralization loss under a carefully stated policy-class embedding.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Excess loss, not merely centralized regret, rises with coupling, horizon, and observation noise; explicit joint-policy commitment reduces excess loss per token more than token-matched dialogue. Refute if rendering/syntax dominates, or teams are near \(V^*_{\mathrm{dec}}\) across both weakly and strongly coupled domains.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Centralized Bayes dominance and information-compression loss for LLM networks are already formalized [S04]. Deliberative Collaboration already uses a solver-computed full-information optimum [S03]. Classical exact Dec-POMDP solution theory is mature [S01, S02], and LLM collaboration is already written as a Dec-POMDP [S05]. Novelty dies if a new paper reports \(V^*_{\mathrm{dec}}\) plus this decomposition; the September search found none. Primary anchors: [S01–S05].

## 5. Solver-certified LLM synthesis and repair of decentralized controllers

**Residual gap.** Formal POMDP controller synthesis and counterexample-guided repair are established. The open object is an LLM emitting a **joint decentralized controller**, whose information legality and value are machine-certified, then repairing only the loss-causing local fragment.

**Precise formulation.** Agent \(i\)’s finite-state controller is

\[
C_i=(Q_i,q_i^0,\gamma_i,\delta_i),\quad
\gamma_i:Q_i\times O_i\to\Delta(A_i),\quad
\delta_i:Q_i\times O_i\times A_i\to\Delta(Q_i).
\]

The joint controller \(C=\otimes_i C_i\) is legal iff no transition or action references nonlocal observations, states, or other agents’ private histories. Exact evaluation is an induced Markov chain over \(S\times\prod_i Q_i\). A verifier returns value \(V(C)\), legality violations, and an occupancy-weighted local loss witness. The LLM is only a proposal oracle; accepted repair \(C'\) must satisfy legality and \(V(C')>V(C)+\eta\).

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** (1) Soundness of the legality checker and exact evaluator. (2) Monotonicity and finite termination of accept-only repair for finite controller spaces and fixed improvement margin. (3) A Bellman/advantage-residual upper bound on \(V^*-V(C)\) localized to controller nodes. (4) Impossibility of completeness under bounded controller size: some optimal history policies require more memory; report regret relative both to \(V^*_{\mathrm{dec}}\) and \(V^*_{m\text{-FSC}}\).

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** At equal token/evaluator budgets, certified local repair reaches lower exact regret than one-shot policy synthesis, free-form online dialogue, and classical iterated local search initialized randomly. Refute if parsing failures dominate, classical local search matches it, or LLM repairs merely rediscover evaluator hints without generalizing across renderings.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Inductive FSC synthesis, symbiotic search/model checking, and sampling-plus-model-checking already occupy the repair template [S07–S09]. Risk-seeking conservative policy iteration already gives monotone local improvement and convergence for compact Dec-POMDP policies [S06]. The claim must be “language-model proposal oracle with decentralized legality and exact comparative evaluation,” not a new synthesis principle. Primary anchors: [S06–S09].

## 6. Incentive-compatible allocation of a scarce audit budget

**Residual gap.** Fleet audit allocation under miscalibrated confidence and human-capacity models now exist, while scored-reporting theory proves strong impossibilities and kinked escapes. No found work combines strategic LLM risk reports, a shared audit-capacity constraint, selective revelation of truth, and a behaviorally binding mechanism.

**Precise formulation.** Each agent \(i\) privately observes risk type \(p_i=P(Y_i=1\mid s_i)\) and reports \(r_i\in[0,1]\). A direct mechanism selects audit probabilities \(x_i(r)\in[0,1]\), \(\sum_i x_i\le B\), and penalties/transfers \(t_i(r,Y_i,M_i)\), where \(M_i\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(x_i)\) and \(Y_i\) is observed only if \(M_i=1\). Reviewer accuracy may fall with total load. Agent utility combines throughput, audit delay, and caught-violation loss. The designer minimizes expected harm plus audit and congestion cost subject to Bayesian IC, individual rationality, and capacity.

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** Prove that if \(x_i=0\) on any report interval and unaudited outcomes are never observed, strict truthfulness on that interval is impossible: reports there are payoff-equivalent or manipulable. An exploration floor \(x_i\ge\epsilon\) enables an importance-weighted proper score and an \(O(1/\epsilon)\) variance / audit-cost trade-off; derive approximate-IC regret under finite samples. Then characterize whether the kinked reserve-report rule remains optimal after coupling agents through \(\sum_i x_i\le B\) and reviewer fatigue. This is sharper than importing the single-agent kink unchanged.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Confidence-ranked auditing induces endogenous under-reporting and can fall below random auditing; a capacity-aware kinked rule separates risk types at fixed budget, while a smooth proper score does not. Refute if real LLM reports do not respond to payoff changes or smooth and kinked rules are behaviorally indistinguishable.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** One Human, N Agents already derives a miscalibration threshold under correlated confidence [S48]; Oversight Has a Capacity already models fatigue [S49]; Endogeneity of Miscalibration and Honest Reporting already supply scored-reporting theory [S50, S51]. The project is derivative unless the capacity-coupled IC theorem and selective-label impossibility are new and the LLM response is real. Primary anchors: [S48–S51].

## 7. Memory validity under teammate co-evolution

**Residual gap.** LLM memory drift is modeled as exogenous environment change or stationary skill decay. Multi-agent self-evolution measures final performance, not whether one agent’s update invalidates another agent’s stored experience.

**Precise formulation.** Augment a POSG with policy-version state \(v_t=(v_{1t},\dots,v_{nt})\) and a provenance-bearing memory pool \(M_t\). A memory item \(m=(\psi,a,\nu,t_0)\) was written under version vector \(\nu=v_{t_0}\). Its current causal value is

\[
\Delta_t(m)=V(\pi_t\mid m\text{ retrieved})-V(\pi_t\mid m\text{ withheld}).
\]

Teammate evolution changes the transition kernel perceived by agent \(i\): \(P_i^{v_{-i,t}}\). Retrieval chooses items under partial knowledge of \(v_{-i,t}\). The objective maximizes reward minus retrieval/evaluation cost while controlling the probability of retrieving an item with \(\Delta_t(m)<0\).

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** Use a simulation/performance-difference lemma to bound value drift by discounted total-variation changes in teammate policies, e.g. \( |V^{v}-V^{v'}|\le C_H\sum_{j\ne i}\sup_h\mathrm{TV}(\pi_j^v(\cdot\mid h),\pi_j^{v'}(\cdot\mid h))\). Derive a churn-aware retention rule minimizing worst-case stale-memory regret. Prove a provenance necessity result: without authorship/version metadata, endogenous teammate drift and exogenous tool drift can be observationally equivalent to the retriever, so no retrieval policy can uniformly choose the correct invalidation rule.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Memory-item hazard increases with teammate version distance after controlling for age, and provenance/churn discounting beats recency and judged-value eviction. Refute if co-evolution hazard is no larger than matched exogenous drift or simple recency is sufficient.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Non-stationarity correction in MARL replay is classical [S30]. GLOVE already addresses memory–environment realignment [S26]; Ratchet gives judge-reliability conditions for skill retirement [S27]; Meta-Team and Group-Evolving Agents already co-evolve teams [S28, S31]. Novelty dies if any of them evaluates item-level counterfactual validity as a function of teammate policy version. Their primary abstracts do not. Primary anchors: [S26–S31].

## 8. Strategic persuasion against correlated verifier panels

**Residual gap.** Individual judges are highly persuadable, panels have strongly correlated errors, and value from extra verification concentrates on pivotal cases. No found work treats the evaluated agent as a budgeted attacker choosing which panel members to pressure after seeing preliminary ballots.

**Precise formulation.** Binary state \(Y\in\{0,1\}\). Judges observe correlated private signals \(X=(X_1,\ldots,X_k)\sim P_\rho(\cdot\mid Y)\) and cast first ballots \(b^0\). An attacker observes allowed side information \(z\) (possibly \(b^0\)) and allocates persuasion effort \(e_j\ge0\), \(\sum_j e_j\le B\). Judge \(j\)’s final ballot follows a response channel \(P(b_j\mid X_j,m_j(e_j),b^0)\). The defender chooses aggregation/escalation rule \(A\) and pays query cost. This is a Stackelberg Bayesian security game; solve

\[
\max_A\min_{e:\sum e_j\le B} P(A(b)=Y)-\lambda C(A).
\]

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** Derive sufficient conditions for a critical panel size \(k^*\): effective independent information saturates with correlation while the attacker’s cheapest pivotal-coalition cost grows sublinearly, making robust accuracy non-monotone in \(k\). Prove an impossibility for anonymous deterministic majority when the attacker can identify and flip a minimum winning coalition. Compare with a margin-aware rule that routes only pivotal profiles to an independent evidence channel. A theorem must state the attacker’s information; full ballot visibility makes the result much easier and less novel.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** Under fixed persuasion budget and pivotal targeting, panel accuracy decreases beyond \(k^*\); independent-evidence escalation restores monotonicity more cheaply than adding judges. Refute if targeted attacks do not outperform random allocation or accuracy remains monotone through \(k=9\).

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Correlated-jury theory already weakens Condorcet gains, and election bribery already formalizes targeted vote changes [S42]. Jagged Judges, Nine Judges, and Blind to the Pivotal Vote provide almost every empirical ingredient [S43–S45]; style attacks and persuasion-override work occupy the attack channel [S46, S47]. This can be rejected as a straightforward composition unless the non-monotonicity theorem and minimax defense are genuinely new. Primary anchors: [S42–S47].

## 9. Harness configuration as leaderboard manipulation

**Residual gap.** One paper shows harness choice can change the winner; another gives a social-choice analysis of benchmark rigging through training/data manipulation. The open formal question is manipulation by choosing the **measurement configuration** while model weights and items remain fixed.

**Precise formulation.** Models \(M\) are candidates, benchmark items are voters, and a harness configuration \(c\in\mathcal C=\prod_{\ell=1}^d C_\ell\) induces scores or rankings \(s_{mjc}\). An evaluator with favored model \(m^*\) chooses \(c\) at cost \(\kappa(c,c_0)\). Define constructive config manipulation and robustness radius

\[
R_F(m^*)=\min_{c:F(s(c))=m^*}\kappa(c,c_0),
\]

and worst-case champion set \(W_F=\{m:\exists c,F(s(c))=m\}\). A robust rule may optimize worst-case regret or distributionally robust performance over admissible \(c\), subject to discrimination loss.

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** Establish complexity of deciding \(R_F(m^*)\le b\) for common rules under factored binary configuration axes; reductions from election bribery or knapsack are plausible but must respect config-wide coupled item flips. Prove that a configuration-invariant strict ranking exists iff the pairwise dominance relation is acyclic and invariant over \(\mathcal C\); otherwise every resolute rule satisfying a responsiveness axiom is manipulable by some config selector. Evaluate minimax or fragility-discounted rules and give a robustness–discrimination Pareto bound.

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** The robustness ordering of arithmetic mean, median, pairwise majority, and mean win rate reverses between training-data manipulation and harness-config manipulation; a fragility-aware rule shrinks \(|W_F|\) without materially reducing stable-item discrimination. Refute if rule ordering is invariant or robustness gains vanish under held-out configurations.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** How Hard Is It to Rig a Benchmark already imports social choice and proves manipulation complexity [S41]; There Is No Neutral Harness already reports winner changes under configuration [S40]; general election bribery supplies the reductions [S42]. Novelty is only the coupled “one config changes many ballots” action model and a robust aggregation rule. If [S41] already permits measurement-instrument manipulation rather than training/data changes, the gap closes; its current scope does not appear to. Primary anchors: [S40–S42].

## 10. Admission gates for self-modifying harnesses under selection pressure

**Residual gap after live check.** This candidate must be narrowed sharply. Gaming Without an Attacker already demonstrates benchmark fingerprinting under LLM-driven selection and states that held-out probes work only on non-enumerable axes [S38]. The remaining opportunity is a **formal comparison of admission mechanisms** for repeated self-modification, not another empirical demonstration of gaming.

**Precise formulation.** A proposer repeatedly chooses candidate harness \(h_t\in\mathcal H\) after observing past gate feedback \(y_{<t}\). A gate \(G\) has finite evaluation budget \(n\), private randomness \(\omega\), and possibly reusable validation data \(D\); it admits \(h_t\) from measured score \(\hat U_G(h_t;D,\omega)\). True deployment utility is \(U^*(h_t)\) under construct distribution \(P^*\). The proposer maximizes admission probability or measured score, not \(U^*\). Define manipulation loss

\[
L(G,\mathcal H,T)=\sup_{\sigma}\mathbb E[\hat U_G(h_\tau)-U^*(h_\tau)\mid h_\tau\text{ admitted}],
\]

where \(\sigma\) is an adaptive proposal strategy. Compare non-regression, task-agnostic constraints, sealed tests, randomized audits, and reusable-holdout-style gates.

**Theorem / impossibility / bound opportunity.** Prove an impossibility: any deterministic public gate over a finite enumerable test support is perfectly gameable by an unrestricted harness class that can condition on test identity. Under bounded description length, differential privacy, or hidden non-enumerable axes, derive a finite-\(T\) upper bound on manipulation loss/max-information. Then solve the designer’s minimax problem trading evaluation cost, false rejection, and manipulation loss. This is where the paper becomes theory rather than a replay of [S38].

**Falsifiable hypothesis.** At matched evaluation cost and proposal budget, standard non-regression gates have larger double-held-out manipulation loss than randomized/limited-feedback gates; pass rate is weakly related or negatively related to true gain. Refute if adaptive-proposal leakage is negligible and simple sealed tests dominate without utility cost.

**Closest theory that could invalidate novelty.** Adaptive holdout reuse already gives generalization guarantees [S39]. HarnessBank, DarwinX, HarnessCompass, and HarnessOpt-Bench already instantiate several gate families [S33–S36]. BenchJack audits benchmark exploitation [S37], while [S38] directly occupies selection-induced fingerprinting. Novelty survives only if the mechanism game and cross-gate bounds are new; otherwise this should be dropped. Primary anchors: [S33–S39].

## Cross-gap theorem priorities

If only three theory projects can be started immediately:

1. **Biased-blame inversion:** smallest model, directly estimable kernel, sharp welfare sign change.
2. **Selective-label impossibility for adaptive verification/auditing:** one theorem can support both gaps 2 and 6, but the papers should not double-claim it.
3. **Central-only non-identifiability:** a short construction that makes the need for \(V^*_{\mathrm{dec}}\) undeniable before any benchmark is run.

Do not lead with equilibrium existence alone, the algebraic regret identity, or a simulation threshold without monotonicity/identification conditions. Those are too easy for closest theory to absorb.

## Bottom line

The safest novelty is in **couplings that existing literatures leave separate**: measured blame errors into sanctions, measured trust behavior into delayed verification control, and strategic contributors into persistent memory dynamics. The least safe claim is harness-gate gaming: [S38] already demonstrates the phenomenon, so only a new formal mechanism/bound remains defensible.
