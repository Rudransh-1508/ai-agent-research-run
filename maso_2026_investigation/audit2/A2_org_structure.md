## 1. Bottom line

**No fatal collision in the org-structure lane, but the lane is much more crowded than the first swarm's reports imply, and two papers substantially narrow claims 1 and 12.**

- **Strongest collision: OrchestraBench (arXiv:2608.05263, 5 Aug 2026)** — fault injection over templated enterprise workflows with **cascade radius measured as a function of pipeline depth (mean 0.9 → 4.7 across depths 3–7)** plus per-failure-mode recovery rates (tool faults 1.0, ambiguous delegation 0.30, latent/semantic 0.0) on real Claude models. This is "depth varied under faults with propagation + recovery measurement" — the exact skeleton of H1 — but on **sequential single-LLM dependency chains**, not organizational delegation/authority trees, and recovery is measured as *rate*, not *cost vs depth*. ABSTRACT VERIFIED (arXiv abs page, this run); consistent with D1's full-text-based record.
- **Q1 answer:** Organizational **delegation depth** (number of authority/hierarchy levels) under injected faults: **NOBODY** in LLM-MAS. Chain/pipeline depth under faults: OrchestraBench. Three-agent linear/flat/hierarchical patterns under faulty agents: Huang/Zhou et al. 2408.00989. Topology shape × size in simulation: Containing the Cascade. The specific dose-response "delegation depth → fault propagation/recovery cost in tool-executing LLM organizations" remains unclaimed.
- **Q2 answer:** Recovery **cost** as a function of depth: **NOBODY** in LLM-MAS. The only depth→cost-inflation measurement remains Dellarocas & Klein ICMAS 2000 (classical, non-LLM). Recovery-cost measurement exists without depth variation (OpsAgent MTTR; Verifier Tax recovery-after-block 21%→~0%; OrchestraBench recovery rates).
- **H3 (scarcity sign reversal): NO collision found in LLM-MAS.** The sign reversal exists only in classical D&K (EH services worse than survivalist under scarce subcontractors, short tasks). But note: "containment benefit is conditional/moderated" results are accumulating fast (Audited Action Gates SSRN 2026; Leadership as Coordination Control 2606.19111; Context/Reasoning/Hierarchy 2605.16205's "deliberation cascade") — H3's *specific* scarcity mechanism is open, its *generic* "it depends" framing is not.

## 4. New collisions/neighbors found beyond prior reports (all verified this run)

1. **OrchestraBench 2608.05263** — see §1. The paper H1 must differentiate from head-on. Its own disclaimer: "controlled-chain mechanism probes, not domain-workload claims"; 26-case diagnostic + mechanism probes; single verifiable-computation domain; no org constructs.
2. **Decomposition Buys Integrity, Not Yield** (arXiv:2609.17464, 15 Sep 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — depth-k decomposition trees; per-level keep-probability C≤1 and per-tier alignment penalty μ=0.939 (1 in 16 briefs off-target) ⇒ per-tier integrity Cμ=0.536; flat optimal for yield; depth cuts root-context exposure N→N^{1/k}; production cost scaling N^1.39; only 0.7–11.3% of sessions worth delegating; hazard model on 743,819 production tool calls. **Quantifies information loss per delegation tier on real traces** — the closest "depth cost" evidence, but no faults, no recovery.
3. **Leadership as Coordination Control** (2606.19111) — supervisory control helps recovery only under contingency conditions (unreliable majority + recoverable task + interaction doesn't self-repair). A **recovery-advantage boundary** for supervision — H2/H3-adjacent moderation result.
4. **Context, Reasoning, and Hierarchy** (2605.16205, 15 May 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — hierarchy × deliberation in adversarial POMDP (CybORG, 3,475 episodes, token-level cost accounting): distributing deliberation across a hierarchy is up to **3.4× worse at 1.8–2.7× more tokens** ("deliberation cascade"); hierarchy without deliberation best. Mechanism-backfires-in-hierarchy evidence — H3-flavored but about deliberation cost, not resource scarcity.
5. **BRA-Audit** (2608.14668, 2 Aug 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — budget-aware runtime auditing of LLM-MAS: audit-point placement under fixed audit-call budget, localized recovery, −17.2% to −40.6% tokens. Containment-under-budget is occupied as an *optimization* problem (not a sign-reversal study).
6. **The Verifier Tax** (2603.19328, 18 Mar 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — runtime safety enforcement on tau-bench: intercepts ≤94% of non-compliant actions but SSR <5%; **recovery after blocked actions 21% → ~0%**; enforcement "imposes a significant verifier tax on conversational length and compute." Single-agent; H2 cost-side evidence.
7. **When Do Audited Action Gates Help?** (SSRN 10.2139/ssrn.7348298, 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED via Crossref) — 1,330 runs, 9 models: verification gates suppress superstition AND discovery; benefit concentrated in weak baselines (+0.31 when baseline <0.60 vs +0.01 at 0.80–0.95). Containment benefit is strongly **moderated** — not scarcity, but the same genre.
8. **DARC — Delegation-Aware Runtime Contracts** (Research Square 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10533072/v1, 13 Aug 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED via Crossref) — budgets/obligations attenuate across authority-bearing handoffs; transitive revocation + lineage quarantine; state recovery. Occupies delegation-chain *enforcement* (sibling-proposal territory), not depth × fault spread.
9. **ResMAS** (2601.04694, 8 Jan 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — RL-designed resilient topology + topology-aware prompts under agent-failure perturbations. Design-time resilience; communication topology only.
10. **OpsAgent** (ICAISET 2026, DOI 10.1109/ICAISET66439.2026.11541300; ABSTRACT VERIFIED via S2 dump) — 1,074 fault-injection scenarios, MTTR −67.3%, governance policy graph, escalation-path validation. AIOps recovery-cost measurement without org-structure IV.
11. **Smarter Saboteurs, Better Fixers** (2606.12709, AIWILD @ ICML 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — linear workflows, model scale × adversarial compromise; terminal Fixer stage collapses 53.7pp drop to 0.6pp. Linear only.
12. **RoMa: Credibility-Aware Fault-Tolerance Framework for LLM MAS** (CSCWD 2026, DOI 10.1109/cscwd68734.2026.11582680; **METADATA ONLY** — IEEE bot-walled, no abstract in Crossref, not on arXiv/OpenReview). Already flagged in FINAL_RECOMMENDATION as occupying attribution→containment. **Verification gap: content unconfirmed; not load-bearing for my classifications.**
13. **Biological Motifs for Agentic Control** (2607.04240; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — theoretical error-amplification/suppression bounds for feed-forward topologies. Theory only.
14. **Hallucination Snowball** (2608.14588, FAGEN @ ICML 2026; ABSTRACT VERIFIED) — per-stage escape probabilities (24.6/48.3/89.3%), boundary gates cut survival 58.4%→16.2%, optimal verification *allocation* ("when you verify matters more than whether"). Linear pipeline, one fault type.

## 6. Unexpected finding

**The hierarchy-value question is actively producing contradictory experimental results in 2026, which is *good* for the candidate:** Loop-Back Authority (flat wins on open-ended work, supervisor = liability when it can only opine) vs. OrgAgent (hierarchy wins +102.7% and −74.5% tokens on SQuAD) vs. 2408.00989 (hierarchy most fault-resilient) vs. Width/Memory/Delay (flat matches hierarchy at equal per-agent memory; refutes a "causal floor" preprint claiming flat MAS have irreducible error floors removable only by hierarchy). The field has **no settled account of when hierarchy helps** — performance, cost, and resilience point in different directions across papers. The candidate's depth × fault × scarcity design is exactly the kind of experiment that could organize this mess; conversely, reviewers primed by any one of these papers will demand the candidate reconcile with the others.
