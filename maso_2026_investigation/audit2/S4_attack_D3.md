# S4-ATTACK-D3 — Adversarial attack on Direction D3: "Detection-quality as the transferred variable"

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Target direction (as assigned):** Classical MAS fault-handling (D&K sentinels, Hägg, Tambe STEAM) assumed perfect, free crash detection; LLM semantic-fault detection is imperfect and costly. Parametrically vary detector quality (oracle → syntactic → LLM-semantic at measured accuracies) and map the detection-quality × containment-benefit frontier on tool-executing LLM organizations, testing which classical predictions survive at each detection level.
**Stance:** hostile. Goal is to find fatal problems while cheap.

## VERDICT: SURVIVES WITH NARROWING

The empirical core — a parametric detection-quality × containment-benefit frontier on real tool-executing LLM organizations, with measured real-detector operating points and attribution-free containment baselines — is verifiably unoccupied (W2-cand3 §3: "No found work anywhere varies attribution accuracy as a controlled dial and measures downstream intervention utility"; DataCite/OpenReview/arXiv-channel negative searches). But the direction **as stated** carries one false premise, one flat-frontier risk backed by the two best existing measurements, one single-agent occupant of its low end, and one internal overlap with a sharper hypothesis (A10-H1). All fixable by narrowing; none fixable by ignoring.

## (a) Is "detection quality mediates containment benefit" already answered by CtC's coverage axis or BRA-Audit's budget ablation?

**Qualitatively: yes, three times over. Parametrically on real LLM orgs: no.**

1. **CtC's contract-coverage axis (FULL TEXT, reports/CtC_fulltext_verification.md).** Table I organizes six fault modes by assigned structural detectability (SCHEMA_VIOLATION 0.95 → HALLUCINATED_FACT 0.22); Table VI shows per-fault containment differences that track those assignments. This *is* "detection quality mediates containment benefit" — in a discrete-event simulation with **zero LLM calls**, where detectability is a **set parameter, not a measured quantity**. CtC occupies the *question's form* and pre-registers the expected ordering (semantic faults hardest) by construction. Hostile read: "CtC already showed containment decreases as detectability decreases." Rebuttal survives in exactly one sentence (already in the verification note): **"CtC assigns detectability; we measure it"** — their Table VI is a hypothesis, D3 is its empirical test. But D3 must cite Table I/VI head-on and frame itself as the measurement of CtC's assumption, or a reviewer pattern-matches and calls it a re-run with LLMs.
2. **BRA-Audit's detector-budget ablation (FULL TEXT via audit2/A1, arXiv:2608.14668).** The ablation varies audit *budget* (ρ ∈ {0.2,0.4,0.6,1.0} — quantity/coverage of auditing), not per-detection *accuracy*. Score rises monotonically; the harm channel is false positives (0%→4.67%→12% FP) causing unnecessary rollbacks. So BRA-Audit occupies **"more detection → more FP-induced containment harm on real LLM MAS"** — a detection-quality-relevant harm channel already documented on the target substrate (albeit adversarial faults, additive budget, no org structure). D3 cannot claim "first to show detection imperfection harms containment on LLM MAS"; it can claim the first *accuracy-parametric* map with organic faults.
3. **Xiang's gate-strictness conditions (ABSTRACT ONLY, SSRN 10.2139/ssrn.7348298, via audit2/A9 R3).** Finding (ii): self-reported verification is worthless; only harness-audited gates help. That is a **two-level detection-quality manipulation with a containment-benefit readout on LLM agents** — D3's low end already has a published datum, with better statistics (1,330 runs, 9 models, pre-registered) than D3's plan. Mitigations: single-agent, no propagation, no organization, strictness is binary not parametric. But a hostile reviewer will say "the strictness-of-detection × gate-benefit relationship is published; you added agents."
4. **Itkin 2606.27409 (PARTIALLY VERIFIED, abs + HTML sections).** Verification *delay/dose* — a detection-quality-adjacent variable — already has closed-form stability thresholds and a backfire result (dose-delay oscillation) on 5 open models. Occupies "detection timeliness mediates containment stability" theoretically; belief-consensus setting, no tools, no org.

**Answer to (a):** the mediation claim is occupied as a qualitative expectation (CtC, by construction), as a budget-side empirical result (BRA-Audit), as a two-level strictness result (Xiang), and as a delay-side theory (Itkin). What is nowhere answered: the **parametric accuracy frontier with real measured detectors, organic faults, propagation containment (not repair), and organizational structure** — and, critically, the **crossover structure** (where containment benefit flips sign per mechanism as detection degrades).

## (b) Is varying a synthetic detector dial too artificial?

**The dial as specified (oracle/noise mixture, scalar a) has a real internal-validity hole; it is fixable and partially mitigated by design elements already in the repo.**

1. **Scalar accuracy is the wrong unit.** Real detector error is *fault-class-conditional and FP/FN-asymmetric*; the synthetic mixture is uniform and symmetric. Measured operating points prove the spread: Who&When lineage 53.5% agent-level vs 14.2% step-level (arXiv:2505.00212, verified); LongRCA best root-step 24.1%; TelemetrySuffBench detection F1 ≈ 1.0 with origin-step accuracy ≤ 0.5% (detection–localization decouple completely); CtC's assigned per-class coverage spans 0.22–0.95; CUADebug machine RCA 19–29% exact (FULL TEXT via W2-cand3). A frontier mapped on scalar *a* may not predict where any real detector lands, because real mid-accuracy detectors fail **on the hardest faults** (accuracy correlates with fault subtlety), while the dial fails **uniformly at random**. The x-axis must be at least 2D (sensitivity × specificity) and reported per fault class, or the frontier is an artifact of the error model.
2. **Correlated-error / common-mode hole.** An LLM-semantic detector shares failure modes with the LLM agents it monitors (same model family, same blind spots — cf. VP-CONTROL's common-mode vote failure, 62.9% vs 22.9%, ABSTRACT VERIFIED via A1). The synthetic dial assumes detector errors independent of fault content and agent errors. At minimum one **real LLM-semantic detector arm** (not mixture) is mandatory as an ecological-validity anchor; the dial then calibrates the axis and the real detectors are validation points. This is already the repo's design intent (W2_statistics §6.1 anchors the dial to injected-fault ground truth; FINAL_RECOMMENDATION §6.2 has oracle + learned-detector arms) — D3's write-up must make the real-detector arms *primary evidence*, not decoration.
3. **The enforcement-gap confound (NEW hostile exhibit).** "Why LLM Agents Collapse Without Oversight" (arXiv:2609.15293, submitted ICLR 2027; abstract-level via C1) *proves* detection quality is irrelevant when enforcement probability ≈ 0. Consequence for D3: the frontier is only meaningful with containment **actuation held fixed and real**; if any arm's containment mechanism has an enforcement gap, the dial measures the gap, not detection quality. Must be cited and designed around.
4. **Statistical feasibility is fine.** W2_statistics §3.6: 6 levels × 40 instances = 240 obs/arm, threshold CI ±0.10–0.20 — powered, provided guided vs attribution-free curves separate by ≥20pp at mid-range; the binding risk is separation at a=1.0 (i.e., the top of the frontier), which is exactly where the CUADebug/conformal evidence says separation may not exist (see §most-damaging).

**Answer to (b):** a *purely* synthetic scalar dial is too artificial and would not survive a hostile methods review; a dial **anchored to measured real-detector operating points, with per-fault-class sensitivity/specificity and at least two real-detector arms (syntactic, LLM-semantic) as validation points**, is defensible and is in fact the only feasible way to get a continuous IV (you cannot summon detectors of arbitrary accuracy).

## (c) What does the classical-transfer framing add beyond a detector-quality ablation?

**It adds three things an ablation does not have — and one liability.**

Adds:
1. **Interpretability of the whole transfer test (this is the big one).** R3's W2 (FULL TEXT of D&K behind it): in D&K's experiments detection of agent death is effectively perfect (polling + timeout); fault type (crash vs semantic) is therefore *perfectly confounded* with detection quality (perfect vs imperfect). Without the oracle-detector arm, **no** H1–H3 result can distinguish "classical theory fails on LLM substrate" from "classical cheap-perfect-detection assumption fails." D3 is not an optional extra direction — it is the de-confounding instrument that makes the paper's other three hypotheses interpretable. This is why the classical mock reviewer named exactly this move as the reject→accept flip (R3 §6).
2. **A falsifiable prediction set with non-obvious content.** "Which classical predictions survive at each detection level" generates quantitative, pre-registerable predictions an ablation lacks: (i) the H3 scarcity-backfire *threshold shifts* as detection quality drops (FPs waste scarce verification budget → backfire onset at higher budgets); (ii) the containment-mechanism *ranking permutes* across detection levels, because mechanisms consume different detection outputs — a verifier gate needs only detection, quarantine needs agent attribution, checkpoint rollback needs step localization (TelemetrySuffBench shows these decouple by orders of magnitude); (iii) A10-H1's non-monotonicity (weak verifier *worse than none* via authority-laundering of false passes). These are the figures a classical reviewer cites.
3. **Reviewer-pool fit.** MASO's pool is classical-MAS-heavy; Kaminka & Tambe's monitoring-selectivity theorems (JAIR 2000, FULL TEXT via A5) are the classical detection-coverage theory, and D3 is its empirical LLM-substrate counterpart. Framed this way D3 *flatters* the lineage instead of ignoring it.

Liability (must be neutralized in the premise sentence):
4. **The premise "classical MAS assumed perfect, free detection" is FALSE as stated, and the MASO pool is exactly the pool that will know it.** Kaminka & Tambe JAIR 2000 (FULL TEXT): monitoring-selectivity theorems (sound+complete centralized detection requires monitoring all teammates, Thm 3; observable partitioning reduces it, Thm 4) and ModSAF experiments with **FP/FN-asymmetric detection heuristics** (coherence: no false positives but false negatives; incoherence: reverse). Klein & Dellarocas CSCW 2000 (FULL TEXT via R3 W1): "diagnoses represent **hypotheses rather than guaranteed deductions**" — detection treated as fallible, type-specific, in 2000. The defensible narrowing (A5's own conclusion): classical **experimental evaluations** (D&K ICMAS 2000) used effectively perfect crash detection and detection **accuracy was never an experimental factor** — not "never considered." One wrong sentence in the intro hands a classical reviewer a strawman kill.

## The most damaging evidence (ranked)

1. **The flat-frontier threat — CUADebug + Conformal null (both FULL TEXT via W2-cand3).** The two best existing measurements of the detection/attribution-quality → intervention-utility relationship both find **near-insensitivity**: CUADebug machine RCA at 19–29% exact ≈ human oracle (28.5% vs 29.9% re-rollout Acc); Conformal Agent Error Attribution (2605.06788) coverage-guaranteed sets beat Top-1 rollback "by a small margin, within one standard deviation." If containment benefit is detection-quality-insensitive across the achievable range, D3 maps a flat line at exactly the operating points real detectors occupy. Saving graces: both are single-agent *repair/re-execution*, not propagation containment in organizations; W2-cand3 used their disagreement as motivation; and a flat frontier is publishable **iff** attribution-free arms (verify-everything, restart-all) are included and the ROC/microreboot counter-thesis (Candea OSDI 2004 — cheap blind recovery dominates expensive diagnosis) is engaged head-on. But this evidence caps the expected effect size and must shape pre-registration: the null/flat outcome is a live possibility, not a tail risk.
2. **Kaminka & Tambe JAIR 2000 + K&D CSCW 2000 (FULL TEXT)** — falsify the stated premise; see (c) liability.
3. **Xiang finding (ii) (ABSTRACT ONLY)** — the strictness × gate-benefit datum already exists on LLM agents; D3's novelty is the parametric extension + propagation + org, not the existence of the relationship.
4. **CtC Table VI (FULL TEXT)** — the expected frontier ordering is already in print as a simulation assumption; D3 must be framed as its test.
5. **Internal overlap: A10-H1** already proposes the verifier-accuracy dial (a ∈ {0,.2,.4,.6,.8,1}) with a *sharper* non-monotone prediction (BR(weak verifier) > BR(no verifier) > BR(strong verifier)). If D3 ships as "map a frontier" without A10-H1's laundering prediction, it is the weaker version of an internal hypothesis. Absorb it.

## What precisely survives

**"The detection-quality × containment-benefit frontier for organic faults in tool-executing LLM organizations: a parametric study (oracle → syntactic → LLM-semantic, anchored at measured operating points) that (i) de-confounds the classical transfer test (separating theory failure from the failure of the cheap-perfect-detection assumption), (ii) tests whether the classical mechanism ranking permutes as detection degrades from localization-capable to detection-only, (iii) measures whether the scarcity-backfire threshold shifts with detector quality, and (iv) tests non-monotonicity (weak verification worse than none) against attribution-free baselines."**

Mandatory conditions:
1. Premise narrowed: "classical *experiments* used effectively perfect crash detection; detection accuracy was never an experimental factor" — cite Kaminka & Tambe JAIR 2000 and K&D CSCW 2000 as the classical fallibility engagement, not as absent.
2. Dial anchored to measured real operating points (Who&When 53.5%/14.2%; CUADebug 19–29%; CtC's 0.22–0.95 as hypotheses); sensitivity × specificity reported per fault class; ≥2 real-detector arms as primary evidence.
3. Attribution-free containment arms (verify-everything, restart-all) included; ROC/microreboot and Pauker-Kassirer threshold lineage cited; flat-frontier outcome pre-registered as publishable.
4. CtC Table I/VI cited head-on ("they assign detectability; we measure it"); BRA-Audit's FP channel and Xiang's strictness finding cited as the occupied low/budget ends.
5. Absorb A10-H1's non-monotone laundering prediction; hold containment actuation fixed (2609.15293 enforcement-gap confound).
6. Cite CUADebug's future-work sentence as the field's own acknowledgment the question is open — it is both the strongest openness evidence and the nearest scoop risk.
