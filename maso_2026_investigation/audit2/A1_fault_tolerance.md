# AUDIT2 / AGENT 1 — LLM-MAS Fault Tolerance Specialist

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Lane:** faulty agents, agent failures, semantic faults, hallucination propagation, error propagation, cascading failures, resilience, robustness, degradation, fault tolerance, recovery, self-repair, multi-agent reliability — in LLM multi-agent systems.
**Stance:** adversarial; trying to kill H1 (org depth changes propagation/recovery cost), H2 (containment reduces propagation at a cost), H3 (containment backfires under resource scarcity — sign reversal), and the "first experimental transfer test of classical MAS fault-handling" framing.

---

## 1. Bottom line

**No EXACT collision found for H1, H2, or H3. But the lane is much more crowded than the wave-1 reports indicate, and H3 in particular is now surrounded on four sides.** I found 11 relevant papers NOT covered by any of the 48 prior reports, including one (BRA-Audit, arXiv:2608.14668) that occupies the "budgeted containment with token-cost accounting and localized recovery in real LLM MAS" territory outright, and one production failure study (Agent Mesh, arXiv:2608.26225) that documents containment mechanisms *backfiring* in the wild (enforcement blocking correct work; bystander damage). The candidate's surviving wedge narrows to: **the controlled factorial experiment** (org structure × containment mechanism × resource regime) measuring propagation and recovery cost on real tool-executing LLM organizations. Nobody has run that triple. But "containment can backfire" as a *phenomenon* is no longer claimable as novel, and "budgeted containment for LLM MAS" as a *design space* is occupied.

**Classification summary (details in §4):**
- H1: NO EXACT/NEAR collision. PARTIAL collisions: Huang 2408.00989 (structure × resilience), MAS-FIRE 2602.19843 (architecture × fault tolerance), CTHA 2601.10738 (hierarchy+authority constraints → 47% cascade reduction), Reliability-Contagion 2607.21912 (connectivity × propagation, real-LLM), Contagion Networks 2606.20493 (topology × preference contagion), Leadership-as-Coordination 2606.19111 (coordination control × recovery contingency). Nobody varies *delegation/organizational depth* and measures *recovery cost*.
- H2: NEAR collision — **BRA-Audit 2608.14668** (budget-aware auditing in real LLM MAS, token-cost + rollback-cost vectors, localized recovery, FP-harm channel from over-auditing). PARTIAL: Hallucination Snowball 2608.14588, CHARM, CtC (simulation), VP-CONTROL 2609.10969, Audit Without Verification 2609.07680, Silence Is Endorsement 2609.20211.
- H3: NO EXACT collision, but PARTIAL collisions on every component: backfire-observed (Agent Mesh 2608.26225, production), backfire-modeled (Oversight Has a Capacity 2606.08919, inverted-U under reviewer fatigue), backfire-in-adjacent-setting (DepthBenchCAD 2609.15122, deeper auditing hurts under fixed evaluation budget), budget-displacement mechanism (RCWT 2607.12216), budgeted-corrector frontiers (2607.09765, 2606.29654). The *sign reversal under scarcity in tool-executing LLM MAS* experiment itself is unclaimed.
- Framing claim ("first experimental transfer test of classical MAS fault-handling"): WEAKENED — Resilient Consensus in Agentic AI (2606.15024) already experimentally tests classical resilient-consensus transfer to LLM agents; EBFT/Honest Quorum (2607.16109) transfers BFT theory; CtC transfers the sentinel pattern in simulation. The *specific* triplet (Dellarocas & Klein sentinels, delegation-depth cost inflation, scarcity sign reversal) remains untransferred experimentally, but "first transfer test" must be scoped tightly.

## 7. Unexpected findings

1. **The backfire evidence is accumulating from independent directions** (production incidents, audit-layer harm, oversight fatigue, evaluation budgeting) — H3's *plausibility* is now well-supported by prior art, which paradoxically both strengthens the paper's motivation and shrinks its novelty. The candidate should present these as convergent motivation, not discover them in review.
2. **BRA-Audit's FP-harm channel** (over-auditing introduces false positives → unnecessary rollbacks, 0%→12% FP with audit ratio) is an *endogenous* harm mechanism distinct from the candidate's *budget-displacement* harm — the paper should test both.
3. **A visible micro-cluster of independent-researcher preprints** (Itkin: 2606.27409 + 2607.09765; the CtC group; several single-author cs.MA items) is actively colonizing budgeted-corrector/verification-stability territory with formal models — scoop risk on formalization of H3 within 1–2 quarters.
