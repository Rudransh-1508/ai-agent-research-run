# Landscape B4 — Cascading Failures and Error Propagation in LLM Multi-Agent Systems

**Investigator:** B4 specialist (adversarial evidence-gathering)
**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Mission:** Map work on error/failure propagation in LLM MAS; determine how propagation is measured; whether "blast radius" or an equivalent quantitative spread metric exists; whether anyone models propagation across *organizational* boundaries (roles, authority/delegation, memory, accountability, human-agent) rather than message chains.

**Verification method:** Every load-bearing paper below was verified by fetching the actual arXiv abstract page, the OpenReview API record, the Crossref DOI record, or the Semantic Scholar paper record — not search snippets. Sources used: arxiv.org/abs pages (FetchURL), api2.openreview.net/notes/search, api.crossref.org, api.semanticscholar.org. arXiv API and dblp were largely unusable during the sweep (shared-IP 429 rate limiting from concurrent swarm agents; dblp behind Anubis bot wall). Items marked ⚠ were verified only at title/venue level (no abstract obtainable).

## 1. Bottom-line judgment

**The seed hypothesis is NOT killed, but it is materially weakened on two specific fronts and must be repositioned.**

1. **The term "blast radius" is already taken — twice — in the 2026 LLM-agent literature**, in exactly the sense the seed proposes (quantitative spread of a local fault):
   - *Containing the Cascade* (ICERA 2026, IEEE) defines **containment rate, cascade depth, blast radius, and mean-time-to-detection** as metrics, plus a fault-injection benchmark (Cascade-Bench) and a circuit-breaker/checkpoint mediator (Sentinel) for tool-using LLM MAS.
   - *DamageFlow / Blast Radius Score (BRS)* (ICML 2026 AIWILD workshop) decomposes blast radius into **resource sensitivity × action impact × propagation × recovery cost** for tool-using agents under attack.
   A MASO submission that presents "organizational blast radius" as a novel *term* will be scooped. A submission that treats these as prior art and attacks what they demonstrably lack (below) is still viable.

2. **Error-propagation dynamics in LLM MAS is now a crowded 2025–2026 subfield** (From Spark to Fire; Hallucination Snowball; Hallucination Cascade; CHARM; PropUQ-MAS; AgentChaos; TIDE; ErrorChain; a 2026 survey). Propagation over **message/dependency graphs and pipeline stages** is well studied, with multiple formalisms (Markov state transitions, per-boundary escape probabilities, amplification factors, uncertainty inheritance).

3. **What remains genuinely open (the defensible residue):**
   - **Nobody models propagation across organizational boundaries as such.** Searches for organizational structure / role hierarchy / authority / accountability × failure propagation in LLM MAS return nothing. All existing propagation models are over message chains, dependency graphs, or knowledge graphs. "Role drift" appears only as one failure *mode* in a taxonomy (Containing the Cascade), not as a propagation *medium*. Authority/delegation-boundary propagation exists only in the *security* framing (SafeFlow: malicious intent fragmented across delegation; DamageFlow: multi-agent relay attacks) — i.e., adversarial, not accidental-fault, and aligned with the already-occupied delegation-invariants territory.
   - **Containing the Cascade is simulation-only** (discrete-event sim "calibrated to reported MAS failure statistics", 590k *simulated* executions; its claimed open artifact — Zenodo record 21314250 — returns **410 Gone**). No real-LLM-MAS fault-injection containment benchmark with spread metrics currently exists with public artifacts. AgentChaos (ASE 2026) does real runtime fault injection but measures pass@1 degradation and diagnosability, not spread/containment.
   - **Recovery-vs-utility trade-off under containment** is barely studied: Sentinel reports success 31.3%→83.7% and containment 46.2%→99.4% but in simulation; DamageFlow reports 87.5% benign utility retention under BlastGuard but in an 80-scenario security pilot.

## 6. How propagation is measured today (metrics inventory)

Quantitative spread metrics that actually exist in the verified literature:

1. **Containment rate, cascade depth, blast radius, MTTD** — Containing the Cascade (ICERA 2026). Definitions only available at abstract level (full text inaccessible; artifact gone). Simulation-derived.
2. **Blast Radius Score (BRS)** — DamageFlow (ICML 2026 AIWILD): decomposed over resource sensitivity, action impact, propagation, recovery cost; scored by deterministic state-diff oracles on *committed* effects. The most rigorous spread metric found, but adversarial-framing and pilot-scale.
3. **Per-boundary escape probability / survival rate / Markov transition matrix** — Hallucination Snowball (2608.14588).
4. **Amplification factor, normalized hallucination score vs cascade depth** — Hallucination Cascade (2606.07937).
5. **Error-propagation reduction %, cascade detection rate** — CHARM (2606.04435).
6. **Evidence-root lineage propagation, testimony adoption rate, higher-order spread, truth-recovery rate** — TIDE (2608.03421).
7. **Population infection rate (% agents reached)** — rumor/fake-news simulations (2502.01450; AgentSociety).
8. **Topology-weighted severity score** — SCHEMA (2608.00711).
9. **Propagated-uncertainty AUROC/PRR** — PropUQ-MAS (2608.22130).
10. **pass@1 degradation under fault injection** — AgentChaos (2608.06790); resilience-as-performance-drop per structure — Huang et al. (2408.00989).

**Gap:** no unified metric that (a) is defined over *organizational* structure (role, authority, memory, accountability, human-oversight boundaries), (b) is measured on *real* LLM MAS executions rather than simulation, and (c) jointly scores spread, containment latency, recovery completeness, and preserved task utility. BRS is closest in spirit but is attack-focused and tool-effect-only.

## 7. Does anyone model propagation across organizational boundaries?

**No, with three partial exceptions, all adversarial or structural-only:**
- SafeFlow (delegation boundaries, malicious intent), DamageFlow (multi-agent relay attacks, tool effects), memory-poisoning work (memory boundary, adversarial).
- Huang et al. 2024 vary *communication topology* (incl. "hierarchical") and show structure changes resilience — but hierarchy there is message routing, not authority/delegation semantics, accountability, or memory scoping.
- "Role drift" is a fault *type* in Containing the Cascade's taxonomy, not a propagation dimension.
- Searches (OpenReview full-text, 2026-09-21): "organizational structure multi-agent LLM failure" → 0 relevant; "role hierarchy error propagation agents" → 0 relevant; exact phrase "organizational blast radius" → **0 hits anywhere**.

## 8. Threat assessment to the seed hypothesis

**Subsumes/kills (partially):**
- "Blast radius" as a named metric for agent failure spread: **taken** (ICERA 2026; ICML 2026 AIWILD). Using the term as-is invites "already defined by X" reviews.
- Fault-injection + containment mediator (circuit breaker, checkpoint rollback, typed message contracts): **taken at architecture level** by Sentinel — but only validated in simulation with a vanished artifact.
- Error-cascade modeling + mitigation on real MAS frameworks: **taken** by From Spark to Fire (2603.04474).
- Verification-placement / boundary-gate prescription: **taken** by Hallucination Snowball & CHARM.

**Does NOT subsume (the defensible core that remains):**
1. Propagation across **organizational** boundaries (role semantics, authority/delegation for *accidental* faults, memory scoping, accountability assignment, human-agent handoffs) as the explicit object of study — empty.
2. A **real-LLM-MAS** (not simulated) containment benchmark with spread metrics and public artifacts — empty (Containing the Cascade is sim-only + artifact 410; AgentChaos has the harness but no spread metrics).
3. The **containment–utility trade-off** characterized empirically across mechanisms (circuit breaker vs rollback vs quarantine vs escalation-to-human) — only simulated or pilot-scale evidence exists.
4. Conditions determining **amplification vs attenuation** (2606.07937's attenuation finding vs 2603.04474's amplification) — an open scientific question the seed could own.

**Recommended repositioning for MASO:** drop "blast radius" as the headline neologism (or explicitly cite and extend BRS/containment metrics); frame as *organizational* failure propagation with real-system fault injection (reuse AgentChaos-style harness), org-topology-aware spread metrics, and recovery-mechanism comparison under utility constraints. Differentiate explicitly from: delegation-invariants proposal (occupied), SafeFlow/DamageFlow (adversarial), Sentinel (simulated), attribution line (Who&When/MAST/DepTracer — diagnosis, not containment).
