# LANDSCAPE B18 — Uncertainty, Confidence, Calibration in Multi-Agent Systems (MAS)

**Investigator:** B18 specialist (adversarial evidence-gathering)
**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Seed hypothesis under test:** "Organizational failure propagation and recovery in LLM multi-agent systems" — when a local fault occurs, what determines its *organizational blast radius* (spread across role, communication, authority/delegation, tool, memory, accountability, and human-agent boundaries), and which containment/recovery mechanisms minimize systemic impact while preserving task utility.

**Assignment question:** Map work on (a) confidence calibration of LLM agents, (b) uncertainty propagation through agent chains, (c) conformal prediction for agents, (d) miscalibrated-confidence faults, (e) trust calibration between agents. **Is miscalibrated confidence studied as a fault that propagates?**

---

## BOTTOM-LINE JUDGMENT

**Miscalibrated confidence is NOT yet studied as a discrete, injectable *fault* whose *organizational blast radius* is measured across the seed's seven boundaries.** The literature is real, active, and splits into three camps that each *bracket* the seed without occupying its specific niche:

1. **Calibration/UQ of LLM agents** — measures and mitigates miscalibration, but treats it as a *property to estimate*, not a fault that propagates through an organization.
2. **Uncertainty/error propagation in MAS** — models how uncertainty and errors travel across agents (PropUQ-MAS, RUPA, Bayesian-network monitoring), but the propagated quantity is *error/hallucination risk*, not *miscalibrated confidence* as the pathogen.
3. **Fault injection + containment/recovery** — MAS-FIRE, Hallucination Snowball, GUARDIAN, and communication-topology work already study fault propagation and containment in LLM MAS, but the faults are hallucinations / cognitive errors / coordination failures, and the "boundaries" are communication topology, **not** the seed's organizational boundaries (role, authority/delegation, memory, accountability, human-agent).

**The seed is PARTIALLY SUBSUMED in its generic "error propagation + containment" skeleton** (that territory is now crowded in 2026), **but its specific differentiating angle — miscalibrated confidence as the injected fault, and blast radius measured across organizational (esp. authority/delegation, accountability, human-agent) boundaries with containment/recovery — is a genuine, defensible gap.** Survival depends entirely on sharp differentiation from MAS-FIRE / Hallucination Snowball / PropUQ-MAS.

## DIRECT ANSWER: Is miscalibrated confidence studied as a fault that propagates?

**No — not in the seed's specific sense.** The closest existing threads, and why each falls short:

- **Uncertainty/error propagation is studied** (PropUQ-MAS, RUPA, SAUP, Bayesian-network monitoring), but the propagated quantity is *error/hallucination risk* for the purpose of *reliability estimation / failure detection* — not *miscalibrated confidence* as an injected pathogen whose spread is measured.
- **Fault propagation + containment is studied** (MAS-FIRE, Hallucination Snowball, GUARDIAN, topology work), but faults are hallucinations / cognitive / coordination errors, and "boundaries" are communication topology or pipeline stages — not the seed's organizational boundaries (role, authority/delegation, memory, accountability, human-agent).
- **Miscalibration is studied as a property** (Confidence Dichotomy, Xiong, Tian, UALA, TC-ECE taxonomy, MARGIN), including the finding that it *varies along a trajectory* and is *coupled* across steps (arXiv:2609.07395) and that *unverified confidence metadata harms downstream synthesis* (LDP) and *overconfident persuasion overrides truth in debate* (CW-POR) — but none of these frame miscalibrated confidence as a discrete, injectable *fault* and quantify its *organizational blast radius* with containment/recovery.

**The demonstrable mechanisms exist in isolation** (overconfidence is real and tool-induced; confidence metadata propagates and can harm; errors propagate and can be contained), **but nobody has yet run the experiment: "inject a miscalibrated-confidence fault at node X, measure its blast radius across role/communication/authority/tool/memory/accountability/human boundaries, and evaluate containment/recovery mechanisms."**

## DOES ANYTHING HERE KILL OR SUBSUME THE SEED HYPOTHESIS?

**Partial subsumption of the generic skeleton — YES. Kill — NO, provided sharp differentiation.**

**Subsumption pressure (cite-and-differentiate, or the seed looks derivative):**
- **MAS-FIRE (2602.19843)** — fault injection + propagation + topology-dependent containment + recovery tiers. If the seed is just "inject faults, measure spread, add containment," it is MAS-FIRE with a different fault list.
- **Hallucination Snowball (2608.14588)** — injected-fault propagation + containment placement ("when you verify matters more than whether"). Occupies "containment mechanism placement."
- **PropUQ-MAS (2608.22130) / RUPA (2608.16002)** — occupy "uncertainty propagation through agent chains/graphs."
- **Conformal Agent Error Attribution (2605.06788)** — occupies "conformal prediction for agent error attribution + rollback recovery."
- **Topology work (2505.23352, GUARDIAN 2505.19234)** — occupies "communication-topology boundary mediates blast radius."

**What is NOT occupied (the seed's defensible niche):**
1. **Miscalibrated confidence as the specific injected fault** — distinct from hallucination/wrong-answer faults. A confidently-wrong agent is behaviorally different from a wrong agent: it suppresses downstream verification, overrides debate (CW-POR), and poisons trust-based routing (MARGIN) and provenance (LDP). This fault type is absent from MAS-FIRE's 15-type taxonomy and from the propagation literature.
2. **Organizational boundaries beyond communication topology** — especially **authority/delegation, accountability, and human-agent** boundaries. The propagation/containment literature is dominated by communication topology and pipeline stages; the *organizational* dimensions (who is delegated authority, who is accountable, when a human is in/on the loop) are largely unmeasured as blast-radius mediators.
3. **Trust calibration between agents as a propagation channel AND a containment lever** — MARGIN (runtime trust calibration) and DiscoUQ (disagreement-based confidence) show inter-agent trust signals exist and are miscalibrated, but nobody studies *trust miscalibration between role-specialized agents* as the vector by which a local fault becomes systemic, nor *trust recalibration* as a containment mechanism.

**⚠ Territory to avoid:**
- **EquiMem (2605.09278)** is literally a "zero-trust memory game" → overlaps **killed candidate #3**. Do not frame the seed around zero-trust/experience-commons memory.
- **Delegation-invariants / A2A-MCP accountability** is occupied by the existing MASO proposal (`maso/policy_preserving_delegation_research_proposal.md`); LDP (2603.08852) and Conformal Agent Error Attribution (2605.06788) also brush this. The seed may *measure* the accountability boundary but should not make "policy-preserving delegation invariants" its contribution.

**Recommended positioning for the seed:** "Miscalibrated-confidence fault injection with organizational blast-radius measurement across role/authority/memory/accountability/human boundaries, and trust-recalibration + targeted-verification containment" — explicitly differentiated from MAS-FIRE (generic faults, topology), Hallucination Snowball (hallucination, pipeline stages), and PropUQ-MAS (UQ estimation, not fault/containment).
