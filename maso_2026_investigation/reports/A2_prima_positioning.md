## 5. Core answer: native vs opportunistic

**Native MASO paper** — the organizational setting is *constitutive*, not decorative:

| Native vocabulary (use) | Opportunistic vocabulary (avoid leading with) |
|---|---|
| organization, enterprise, workflow, business process | benchmark, SOTA, leaderboard |
| roles, decision rights, authority, delegation | "agents" as bare LLM calls in a loop |
| accountability, responsibility, liability, governance, compliance | prompt engineering, chain-of-thought |
| socio-technical system, hybrid human–agent workforce | single-agent tool use |
| cascading/systemic failure, resilience, containment, recovery | accuracy-only evaluation |
| organizational testbed, field study, deployment, lessons learned | "we propose a framework" with no organizational construct |
| norms, policies, oversight, escalation, audit | MCP/OAuth mechanics as the contribution itself |
| coordination, negotiation, argumentation, BDI (MAS grounding) | agentic-AI buzzwords with no MAS or org-theory grounding |

A native paper: (1) states an **organizational research question**; (2) uses MAS concepts (roles, norms, coordination, delegation) as the analytical frame; (3) treats LLMs as the *implementation substrate* of agents, not the contribution; (4) evaluates with **process + outcome + governance metrics** in an organizational testbed; (5) speaks to human decision rights and oversight. The existing repo proposal (policy-preserving delegation) already models this register.

## 6. Implication for the seed hypothesis (organizational blast radius / failure propagation & recovery)

- **Nothing in venue intel kills or subsumes the seed hypothesis — the venue actively invites it.** MASO topic 7 verbatim includes "detection and mitigation of cascading, and systemic failures"; topics 6 (accountability/authority), 8 (organizational testbeds, reliability metrics), and 4 (human decision rights) complete the frame. "Organizational blast radius" is almost a literal instantiation of the CFP language.
- PRIMA 2025's main track already published resilience-adjacent MAS work (EO-satellite constellation resilience; trust/collusion detection) — so the *technical* lineage exists at PRIMA, but the *organizational* framing is precisely what MASO was created to host. This is the differentiator vs an "opportunistic Agentic-AI" submission.
- Organizer taste (Najjar/Mualla: explainability + trust calibration; van der Torre: norms; Carli: governance) suggests the paper should include **attribution/explanation of failure propagation** (why did the blast radius extend here?) and **governance levers** (who had authority to contain it?), not just fault-injection engineering.
- Differentiation duty vs the occupied delegation-invariants proposal: lead with **failure dynamics, containment, and recovery across organizational boundaries** (role/communication/tool/memory/human boundaries), not with delegation policy preservation.
