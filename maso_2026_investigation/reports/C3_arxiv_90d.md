# C3 — arXiv 90-Day Deep Sweep (Jul–Sep 2026): Failure Propagation, Containment & Recovery in LLM MAS

**Investigator:** Novelty Hunter C3
**Date:** 21 September 2026
**Mission:** Test the seed hypothesis — "organizational failure propagation and recovery in LLM multi-agent systems" (organizational blast radius across role, communication, authority/delegation, tool, memory, accountability, human-agent boundaries; containment/recovery mechanisms) — against the newest preprint literature.

## Method

- **Primary corpus:** Semantic Scholar bulk-search API, `publicationDateOrYear=2026-06-01:2026-09-21`, 38 query strings covering: error cascade(s), cascading failure(s), fault injection, failure/error propagation, blast radius, containment, quarantine, rollback, checkpoint recovery, organizational multi-agent benchmark, hallucination cascade/propagation/snowball, reliability/resilience/fault tolerance, error accumulation, failure modes, systemic failure, error correction, Byzantine faults, domino/ripple/chain-reaction synonyms, accountability/blame attribution, authority/delegation failure, circuit breaker, immunization, human-agent recovery. **552 unique papers** retrieved (`raw/c3_s2_hits.json`, `c3_s2_hits2.json`, `c3_s2_hits3.json`).
- **arXiv API cross-check:** 28 date-bounded queries (`submittedDate:[202606010000 TO 202609212359]`) — largely defeated by API rate-limiting under swarm load; S2 (which indexes arXiv with ~1–2 day lag) used as the comprehensive replacement. Raw scripts/logs in `raw/c3_*.py`, `raw/c3_*.log`.
- **Verification:** every load-bearing paper below was inspected on its actual arXiv abstract page (arxiv.org/abs/...) or via S2/Crossref DOI records — not search snippets. 20 papers verified this way.

## Bottom line

**The generic "error cascade / failure propagation in LLM MAS" framing is DEAD as of Q3 2026 — the space is crowded. One paper ("Containing the Cascade", ICERA 2026) already claims the exact seed framing: containment metrics including the literal term "blast radius", a fault-injection benchmark, and a reference mediator. It does NOT kill the seed, because it is simulation-only (no real LLM calls), published in a low-tier venue, and has zero organizational dimensions (no authority, delegation, accountability, or human-agent boundaries). The defensible core that remains: (a) organizational boundary types as the unit of blast-radius analysis, (b) recovery (not just containment/detection), (c) real LLM MAS execution rather than simulation. Novelty must be claimed on those axes explicitly, and "Containing the Cascade" must be cited and differentiated head-on.**

## Gap analysis — what the 90-day window leaves open

1. **No real-LLM-MAS organizational blast-radius study exists.** The one paper claiming "blast radius" + containment metrics + fault injection (T1) is pure discrete-event simulation with no organizational semantics. Emergence World (A2) is real and long-horizon but has no organizational structure and no recovery.
2. **Recovery is the weakest-covered axis.** Detection/attribution: many papers (R4–R6, B-line). Containment: several (T1, A3, A9, A10, R7). Recovery: only reruns/resampling (R1: 6.9% repair unguided), checkpoint rollback (R7, T1), single-agent replanning (R2). **No organizational recovery: re-delegation, escalation to humans, role reassignment, authority revocation as recovery primitives.**
3. **Boundary coverage is fragmented.** Memory (B1), communication/handoff (B2), authority (B3), accountability (T2, B5), human-agent (B4, Hard to Halt) — each studied in isolation. **No unified multi-boundary blast-radius measurement.**
4. **The organizational layer exists but is unwired to failure.** IMACS (A8) makes org structure executable and shows accountability placement matters — for performance. Nobody has injected faults into an IMACS-style organizational substrate and measured cross-boundary spread + recovery.
5. **Venue signal:** EMNLP 2026 Main (R2, R5), ASE 2026 (A1), ICML 2026 FAGEN workshop (A3) — top venues are publishing agent-failure work now; the window for "first" claims on generic cascades is closed.

## Does anything here kill or subsume the seed hypothesis?

**Kill: No. Subsume: partially, at the framing level.**
- The phrase-level novelty of "blast radius / containment metrics / fault-injection benchmark for LLM MAS" is **gone** (T1, plus A1–A3). A proposal pitched as "we measure failure propagation and build a containment benchmark" would be scooped.
- The seed survives — with real novelty — **only** if centered on what T1/A1–A10 all lack: (i) **organizational boundary types** (role, authority/delegation, accountability, human-agent) as the measured units of spread, on (ii) **real LLM MAS** (not simulation), with (iii) **recovery mechanisms** (organizational repair, not just checkpoint rollback) and (iv) the **containment-vs-utility tradeoff** made explicit. T1 must be cited and differentiated (simulation vs real; message-graph vs organizational semantics). T2 (Audit Without Verification) must be cited on the accountability axis; A8 (IMACS) is a natural substrate to build on.
- Collision risk to monitor: the delegation-invariants territory (existing MASO proposal) is further occupied by MasDrift (B3) and semantic-replay work (B6) — keep the seed away from pure authorization-preservation framing.
