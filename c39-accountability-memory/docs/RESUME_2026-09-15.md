## 1. The LLM experiment: analysed, with one honest failure

**Status: 32 episodes, exactly 4 per profile, analysed. Not abandoned, not
finished.** The run died at episode 33 (machine slept, then memory pressure);
episodes save as they complete, so nothing was lost.

### What stands

Attribution error, bits wrong of 3, over 32 episodes:

| record | bytes | error |
|---|---|---|
| full_trace | 2585 | **0.406** |
| per_agent_summary | 639 | 0.312 |
| artifact_multiset | 2555 | 1.375 |
| global_summary | 598 | 0.875 |
| final_output | 513 | 1.281 |

`full_trace` and `artifact_multiset` are **the same reviews**, ~30 bytes of
`Reviewer N:` labels apart. The gap is **0.969 bits, 95% CI [0.625, 1.281]**,
excluding zero. That is the identity-versus-volume claim on real model output and
it is the number to put in the paper.

### What failed, and why it is not the model's fault

**L5's registered failure condition fired: gamma(full_trace) = 0**, so the margin
analysis is reported INCONCLUSIVE, as promised in advance. L1-L3 read as
falsified and L4 as not evaluable, but none of that means anything while the
estimator is broken.

gamma is a *minimum over agents* of an LP on the estimated channel. For agent 2
with teammates 010 the judge returned (0,1,1) in all four episodes both with and
without that agent's effort, so d_i = 0 exactly and gamma collapses. Seven such
exact-tie cells exist at four episodes per profile. Ties can only push gamma
**down**, never up -- it is a one-sided artefact, not a measurement. `D_judge`
averages rather than minimises, which is why the attribution result survives.

## 5. Four predictions have already been falsified

Worth remembering, because the instinct is to re-derive them:

* **P4a** — an aggregate count's margin is exactly `1/n`, not 1. Mixtures of
  teammate behaviour telescope and cancel the intermediate shifts.
* **P5** — margin is *not* monotone in observation noise, and noise can *create*
  margin where a deterministic record had none.
* **E4, E5** — the coordinator's ship/no-ship bit is not worthless, and
  behaviour-dependent retention keeps far more margin than fixed truncation.
* **H1a, H1b** — the identity price is 1.07–1.59 in the worst matched-storage
  pair, not the >3 predicted, and it does not grow with n.

All are reported as falsified in the paper rather than quietly dropped.
