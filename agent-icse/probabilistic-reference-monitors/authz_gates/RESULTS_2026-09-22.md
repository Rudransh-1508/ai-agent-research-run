# Results: Probabilistic Reference Monitors study

Dates: corpus 2026-09-21; benchmark 2026-09-22 (23:24-00:00 UTC+local). Model: `jev-1.13.0` pinned
(echo-verified on every call). Gateway: TypeSafe direct. 2,210 successful calls, 751,824 input
tokens, **total cost $0.0316**. Raw data: `artifact/` (every call logged with request, response,
model echo, latency; results regenerable via `--replay`).

## Corpus study (P1-P4) — 31 gates, pinned SHAs, hand-verified

| Prediction | Full corpus (n=31) | Held-out (n=15) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1: >=50% fail open on >=1 failure mode | 16/31 = 52% [0.35, 0.68] | 6/15 = 40% | Supported full; NOT in held-out |
| P2: >=60% threshold constants undocumented | 69/88 = 78% [0.69, 0.86] | 74% | Supported |
| P3: >=1 gate ships vendor-illustrative 0.9/0.5 | 8 gates | 4 gates | Supported |
| P4: no cross-evaluation | 0 found | 0 found | Supported |

Key structural findings:
- **Fail-open is a deliberate, documented posture in hook-style gates** ("a dead API must not
  freeze the agent"); CI-style tools fail closed because non-zero exit fails the pipeline.
  Deployment context determines failure semantics.
- **jev-harness fabricates model answers**: on connection failure it substitutes a regex-based
  simulated Jev response and the gate acts on it (client.rs:189-192). Unique in the corpus.
- 8 gates ship the vendor docs' illustrative thresholds verbatim; the vendor docs themselves say
  thresholds must be domain-tuned.

## Benchmark (P5-P8) — 346-item rule-generated corpus + 80 held-out adversarial

### Noise floor (50 items x 10 identical repeats, noul_vendor_0.9)
- p_safe spread: mean 0.025, median 0.02, p95 0.06, max 0.06
- **Decision flip rate: 12% (6/50 items change allow/ask/deny across identical repeats)**
- Spread highest on ambiguous items (mean 0.032)

### Per-gate accuracy by tier (main corpus)

| Gate | clearly_safe | clearly_destructive | ambiguous | adversarial | ECE | AURC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| noul @ vendor 0.9 | **0.0%** | 83.3% | 75.0% | 74.2% | 0.230 | 0.250 |
| noul @ 0.5 | 97.9% | 100% | 0.0% | 43.9% | 0.130 | 0.195 |
| choice 3-way | 100% | 64.7% | 33.3% | 43.9% | 0.094 | 0.148 |
| score 5-level | 35.1% | 83.3% | 28.6% | 43.9% | 0.461 | 0.377 |

### Held-out adversarial (unseen attack families, n=80)
noul@0.9: 60.0% | noul@0.5: 68.8% | choice: 60.0% | score: 80.0% — no collapse on unseen attacks.

### Prediction outcomes

- **P5 (ambiguity gap >= 15 pts): supported for 3/4 gates.** Pooled-clear vs ambiguous gaps:
  noul@0.5: 99.0 pts; choice: 48.3 pts; score: 31.6 pts. Exception: noul@0.9, where the safe-tier
  degeneracy (0% allow) inverts the comparison.
- **P6 (vendor 0.9 not loss-optimal): SUPPORTED.** Chow-optimal threshold differs from 0.9 across
  the whole loss grid (L in {5..100}, c in {0.05..0.5}); vendor threshold significantly worse in
  realized loss, Holm-corrected p ~= 0 in most cells. Separately: **the vendor's 0.9 auto-allows
  0% of safe traffic** — as an auto-allow gate it is operationally a deny/ask-everything gate.
- **P7 (confidence blindness): mixed, more interesting than registered.** noul gate: adversarial
  items get *higher* mean confidence than clean (0.803 vs 0.721) — confidence blindness in its
  strongest form. choice gate: the 5/66 successful injections had low mean confidence (0.156) —
  partial discrimination. Confidence-as-detector works for one primitive and anti-correlates
  for another.
- **P8 (exploratory):** per-primitive calibration differs sharply (ECE: choice 0.094, noul 0.13-0.23,
  score 0.461) — consistent with the grey literature's per-primitive miscalibration finding
  (Score worst), now measured on an authorization corpus.

## Threats to validity (for the paper)

- Corpus labels derive from generation rules; disputable items flagged and excluded from loss
  analysis (`--include-disputable` to override). Ambiguous-tier "correct = ask" is a policy choice.
- Benchmark measures our gate configurations, not each deployed gate's exact question wording.
- Single model version, single evening of measurements; rate limits were stable during the run.
- Convenience sample of early-adopter gates (see corpus/CORPUS_NOTES.md).
